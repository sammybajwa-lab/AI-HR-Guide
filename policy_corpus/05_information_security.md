# MF-POL-301 — Information Security and Data Handling

**Owner:** Information Security  
**Effective:** 15 January 2026  
**Policy version:** 5.0

## 1. Data classes
Morrowfen uses four practical data classes: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Public information has been approved for external release. Internal information is for routine company use. Confidential information could harm the company, customers, or employees if disclosed. Restricted information carries the highest handling requirements and includes credentials, regulated personal data, certain customer datasets, security keys, and information specifically labeled Restricted.

When a document contains more than one class, handle it at the highest applicable class.

## 2. Approved systems
Company information must be stored and shared using approved company systems. Personal email, consumer file-sharing accounts, and unapproved messaging services must not be used for Confidential or Restricted information. Employees should not upload company information into public AI services unless the service is specifically approved for that data class and use case.

## 3. Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for supported company systems. Passwords must not be shared. Password managers approved by Information Security should be used where available. Employees must not approve an MFA prompt they did not initiate.

## 4. Endpoint controls
Company-managed laptops must keep endpoint protection, disk encryption, and operating-system updates enabled. Employees may not disable security controls to install software or improve performance. A lost or stolen device must be reported immediately through the security incident channel.

Restricted data should not be stored locally unless the business process specifically requires it and the device is managed. Temporary local copies should be removed when the business need ends.

## 5. Remote and travel use
When working away from a company site, employees should use a private network or company-approved secure connection. Public Wi-Fi can be used only with required security controls such as the company VPN where directed. Employees must shield screens and conversations from casual observation.

International travel can introduce additional restrictions. An approved remote-work request does not itself waive Information Security requirements. Security may issue a travel device or restrict access to selected systems.

## 6. Customer and regulated data
Customer contractual requirements can be stricter than Morrowfen's general policy. A project team must follow the stricter control. Restricted customer data must not be copied to personal devices, removable media, or unapproved collaboration tools.

## 7. Generative AI and external tools
Employees may use approved generative-AI services for approved business purposes. Before sending information to an external model, the employee must confirm that the service is approved for the relevant data class. Restricted data may not be submitted unless Information Security and Legal have explicitly approved that workflow.

Generated content must be reviewed by a responsible employee before it is used for a material business decision, customer communication, legal statement, financial statement, or personnel action. AI output is not a policy source of record.

## 8. Security incidents
Report suspected phishing, credential exposure, lost devices, malware, accidental sharing, or unauthorized access as soon as discovered. Do not wait to determine whether harm occurred. The Security Response team decides severity and next steps.

Employees should preserve relevant evidence and avoid deleting messages or files related to an incident. They should not contact an attacker or attempt independent forensic investigation unless directed.

## 9. Minimum-necessary access
Access is granted based on job need. Employees must not use access they happen to have for curiosity or unrelated work. Managers review access when roles change. Access should be removed promptly when no longer needed.

## 10. Policy questions
If a requested action involves an unlisted application, uncertain data classification, or an unfamiliar destination, the correct response is to obtain a security review rather than assume the action is allowed.
## 11. Practical handling examples
A spreadsheet containing employee names and compensation is Confidential and belongs in an approved company repository, not a personal drive. A file containing credentials or security keys is Restricted and should not be pasted into chat, tickets, or an AI prompt. A public press release remains Public after publication, but an unreleased draft can still be Confidential. When a customer labels a dataset more strictly than Morrowfen would, the customer restriction controls for that work.

## 12. Exceptions and reviews
A security exception must identify the business need, affected system or data, requested duration, compensating controls, and accountable owner. Approval is time-limited unless Security states otherwise. A past exception does not create a standing right to repeat the same activity after the exception expires.
