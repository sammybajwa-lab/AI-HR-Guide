Usage notes

The date list identifies paid company holidays for U.S.-based regular employees. Treatment of holidays during PTO, required coverage, floating holidays, and emergency closures is governed by MF-POL-102. Employees assigned to another employing entity may have a different calendar. A temporary trip does not change the assigned holiday calendar.

Floating holidays

Two floating holidays are available under MF-POL-102 for eligible regular full-time employees. Floating holidays are requested through the ordinary time-off workflow and are not fixed calendar dates.

Changes

People Operations may republish this calendar if a date is corrected or a company closure is added. The version posted in the controlled policy repository is the source of record.
