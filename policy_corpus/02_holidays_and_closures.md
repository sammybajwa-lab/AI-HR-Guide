# MF-POL-102 — Holidays and Office Closures

**Owner:** People Operations  
**Effective:** 1 January 2026  
**Applies to:** U.S.-based regular employees  
**Policy version:** 2.0

## 1. Company holiday calendar
Morrowfen Systems publishes a U.S. company holiday calendar each year. The current calendar is maintained as a separate controlled document so dates can be updated without rewriting this policy. A company holiday is paid for regular employees who are normally scheduled to work that day, subject to local law and employment classification.

The holiday calendar is not the same as a client holiday calendar. Employees supporting a client, distribution center, or international team may need coverage on a Morrowfen holiday. Managers must identify required coverage in advance and arrange equivalent time off or other treatment consistent with local law.

## 2. Floating holidays
Regular full-time employees receive two floating holidays each calendar year. Regular part-time employees scheduled at least 20 hours per week receive a prorated amount. Floating holidays are available on 1 January for employees active on that date and on the first day of employment for later hires.

A floating holiday must be requested through the normal time-off workflow and requires manager approval. It should normally be requested at least 2 calendar days in advance. Unused floating holiday hours expire at the end of the calendar year unless applicable law requires otherwise. They do not carry over under the company plan.

## 3. Holiday during PTO
A company holiday that falls during an approved PTO period does not use PTO hours. Only hours that the employee otherwise would have worked are charged. An employee with a non-standard workweek should use the schedule actually assigned for that week.

## 4. Emergency office closure
Morrowfen may close a site because of weather, utility failure, safety concerns, civil emergency, building access issues, or a similar event. Site leadership and Workplace Operations decide whether a physical site is closed. A building closure does not necessarily mean all work stops.

Employees who can work safely and effectively from an approved remote location may be asked to do so. Employees whose jobs require on-site work will receive instructions from their manager. Pay treatment depends on classification, scheduled hours, and applicable law. Managers must not tell an employee to use PTO for a company-directed closure without checking the applicable pay rule.

## 5. Early release
An early release announced by an executive or site leader is company-paid time for the affected scheduled hours unless the announcement says otherwise. Employees already on PTO for that day do not receive those PTO hours back unless the announcement explicitly makes the release retroactive for employees already absent.

## 6. Religious and cultural observances
Employees may use floating holidays, PTO, or request a reasonable accommodation for a sincerely held religious observance. Managers should not make judgments about whether an observance is familiar or widely practiced. Accommodation requests are handled under MF-POL-702.

## 7. International and multi-state teams
Employees follow the holiday schedule assigned to their employing entity and work location, not the location of their manager. An employee temporarily traveling does not automatically switch holiday calendars. A formal transfer or written assignment may change the applicable calendar.

## 8. Questions
The annual date list is authoritative for which dates are company holidays. This policy explains treatment of those dates. When the date list and this policy appear inconsistent, People Operations should be asked to resolve the discrepancy before a payroll or attendance decision is made.
## 9. Scheduling examples
A Monday-to-Friday employee whose approved vacation spans the Thanksgiving closure uses PTO only for scheduled non-holiday workdays. An operations employee scheduled on a company holiday follows the coverage plan issued by the manager; the manager must confirm the applicable pay or alternate-time treatment rather than improvising it. A remote employee visiting another state for a week keeps the holiday calendar tied to the employee's employing entity unless a formal assignment changes it.

## 10. Administration
People Operations publishes the calendar before the start of each year when practical. Payroll uses the employee's assigned calendar and schedule to determine paid holiday hours. Questions about whether a date is a holiday should be answered from the controlled annual calendar, not from a general internet calendar or a prior year's list.
